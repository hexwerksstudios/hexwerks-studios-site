HexAxis

The articulated printable flight-stand platform being developed for Death Spiral.

HexAxis holds aircraft above the table and carries numbered reference faces in its printed base. The mechanics and assembly language are taking shape alongside the game.

The stands and mounts shown here remain prototypes. Physical aircraft miniatures in Death Spiral's official 1/144 scale can be found in the official Hexwerks Studios eBay store; check each listing for its exact contents.

System purpose

A stand that communicates, not just supports.

HexAxis holds an aircraft in play and supports clear tabletop reference. It is being built alongside Death Spiral rather than added as a generic stand after the fact.

The prototype photographs show the working relationship between aircraft, upright supports, and numbered bases. More detail will arrive with the next stage of the stand's development.
