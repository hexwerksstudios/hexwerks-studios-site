Death Spiral

A 3D-printable WWI air combat tabletop system.

Take command of a pilot and aircraft in fast, dangerous dogfights where position, nerve, aircraft handling, and pilot skill matter more than dense simulation. Death Spiral combines accessible air combat rules with a connected printable tabletop ecosystem.

Physical 1/144-scale resin aircraft miniatures are listed in our official eBay store. The tabletop game remains in development.

The game

Readable dogfights. Named pilots. Dangerous choices.

Death Spiral is a tabletop WWI aircraft combat game designed around dramatic, readable dogfights. It focuses on pilot identity, aircraft character, energy, positioning, and risk without presenting itself as a dense flight simulator.

The wider design supports campaigns and legendary aces while keeping the immediate table state legible.

01

Position. Where an aircraft is placed, faced, and committed should be clear across the table.

02

Pilot identity. Pilots and historical aces give the aircraft a human story and a distinct table presence.

03

Aircraft character. Different aircraft should feel recognizably different without burying the dogfight in procedure.

04

Risk. Energy, handling, and nerve create decisions that can put a pilot in control or into a death spiral.
