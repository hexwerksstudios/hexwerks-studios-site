## Remaining Death Spiral Media

The repository does not currently contain verified:

- Approved product photographs matched to each of the four verified eBay aircraft listings
- HexAxis engineering diagrams and assembly photography
- Hexlink connection and assembly documentation
- Printable accessory photography
- Assembly diagrams

Where approved photography is unavailable, pages use structured editorial states instead of generated placeholders or stock aviation photography.

The four verified 1/144 aircraft listings use text-only purchase cards. Existing approved Death Spiral artwork and prototype photographs remain in their game and development context; they do not establish a sale item's appearance, scale, finish, or included stands and terrain. Product photos and listing contents are available through the official eBay storefront.

## Image Standards

- Prefer authentic painted models, physical prototypes, test prints, boards, terrain, or approved CAD renders.
- Store images in a folder named for the game, system, or aircraft.
- Use descriptive filenames rather than camera-roll names.
- Provide dimensions for important images when practical.
- Add descriptive alt text for informative images and empty alt text for decorative duplicates.
- Use `loading="lazy"` below the fold.
- Preserve aircraft silhouettes and avoid aggressive crops.
- Compress assets before publication without destroying miniature or print detail.

## Rights

Do not publish third-party model or terrain files without explicit distribution rights. Existing From the Werks and Forge Files licensing notes remain the authority for credits and file distribution.
