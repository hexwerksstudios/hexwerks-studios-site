# Content Requirements

This internal checklist keeps public pages clear about what is known today and what still needs studio material. It is not visitor-facing copy.

## Death Spiral

- Confirm individual aircraft names, variants, nationalities, historical periods, tabletop roles, and approved images before adding Hangar records.
- Confirm the intended tabletop scale and any print or durability guidance before publishing technical notes.
- Confirm HexAxis attachment details, movement references, altitude treatment, and assembly guidance before describing mechanics.
- Confirm which rules, print files, reference sheets, and assembly guides are cleared for public release.
- Confirm the relationship between Hexlink, tile connection or assembly work, the Death Spiral Battlefield, and any broader Hexwerks terrain platform before expanding naming.

## Development

- Add article images only when they show real workshop work, print tests, prototypes, or approved studio art.
- Add dates, versions, outcomes, and playtest notes only when they can be verified from studio records.

## Raven-Scribe

- The Raven-Scribe remains a read-only assistant. Its knowledge sources must stay curated and must not imply product availability, release dates, prices, shipping, or unpublished downloads.
- The current name fits the studio's mythic language but is closely associated with the older games. Decide later whether a studio-wide assistant should receive a more neutral identity; no rename is part of the current site pass.
